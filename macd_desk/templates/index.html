Indicative NSE options rates — re-check against the live Upstox tariff before using these figures for accounting.

Premiums, strikes and deltas come from the live Upstox option chain; the blotter holds only trades the engine actually executed. Nothing on this page is sample or simulated data. Charge figures are computed in Python from the rate card and follow the published NSE/Upstox structure for equity & index options — a planning aid, not a contract note.
